# Pocket Parking – Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Pocket Parking mobile game ("the app") uses and why. The app is published by the developer reachable at [decodelabsofficial@gmail.com](mailto:decodelabsofficial@gmail.com) ("we").

## Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels cleared, scores, gems, settings, your invite code and fuel timer) is stored locally in the app’s private storage. Your operating system may include this data in device backups. We do not run accounts, logins or a game server, and we do not receive this data. You can export or import a backup file yourself from Settings. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app details, and how you interact with ads, to deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Ads are requested as non-personalised where the app controls this setting.

Where required, including in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app uses Google’s consent tool before requesting ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

Learn how Google uses this information: [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings.

## App updates

The app can check for compatible software updates through Expo’s EAS Update service. These requests include information needed to select an update, such as the app version and platform. The service also receives network information such as your IP address when your phone connects. These requests do not include your saved game progress or invite code.

## Notifications

If you turn on the evening reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your phone. No data is sent to us to do this.

## Sharing your invite code

When you tap "Share invite", your phone's share sheet opens with a message containing your invite code. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your choices

 - Manage ad consent in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

 - Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

 - Delete all game data by uninstalling the app.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
